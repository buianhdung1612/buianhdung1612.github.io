About Me

Building Reliable Software Systems.

I'm a software engineer passionate about building reliable, maintainable, and scalable applications, with a strong focus on backend engineering, system design, and full-stack development.

I believe great software is not just about making things work — it's about understanding why they work, how they fail, and how they can evolve over time.

My interests include software architecture, data consistency, application performance, security, and the engineering decisions behind reliable systems.

Beyond building software, I continuously explore new technologies, document what I learn, and share practical engineering knowledge through my technical blog, Dev Journal.

Engineering Journey

From building features to understanding systems.

01 · Build

Shipping a complete product

With TeddyPet I built a product end to end: a Next.js storefront and admin console on top of a NestJS commerce API and PostgreSQL.

02 · Fail

Learning where systems break

Most of the engineering effort went into the failure cases: concurrent checkouts, payment results that arrive late, sessions that must be revoked. Handling them is where backend engineering and data consistency became my focus.

03 · Split

Drawing service boundaries

Media and support chat run as separate services with their own data, which turned service contracts, signed tokens and real-time delivery into concrete decisions with trade-offs.

04 · Teach

Writing to understand

I built Dev Journal, a teaching blog with its own CMS, to explain how software works beneath the surface. Explaining a topic clearly is the best test of understanding it.

05 · Now

Going deeper

Studying software architecture, system design and database internals, and looking for a team where I can apply them to meaningful problems.
